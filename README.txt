See who is at your door the moment they ring. The official Aqara app does not support the G4, and the HomeKit Controller app can't show its live video. This app connects to the doorbell directly over your local network and gives you all of it: a press starts your Flows straight away, complete with a snapshot of your visitor, and you can watch the live picture on your phone, in the Homey web app or on a dashboard, on as many screens at once as you like.

Motion shows up in Homey too, and the chime and its volume, night vision, the status light and the camera itself can be controlled from Homey, all without the Aqara cloud. The doorbell can be connected to only one HomeKit controller, so remove it from Apple Home or the HomeKit Controller app first. Then add it in Homey with the HomeKit setup code, or simply scan the QR code on the doorbell.
